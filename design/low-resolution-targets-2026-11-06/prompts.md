# Imagegen prompts

工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`。全部输出为不透明背景；没有使用 CLI/API fallback。以下为实际使用的提示词，按调用顺序保存。生成结果是设计参考，不是当前软件的能力展示。

## 1. First 32 px concept

Input: `public/illustration.png`. Output: `ai-reference-32-raw.png`.

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: a single reference sprite for evaluating a high-resolution-to-pixel-art converter. Input image 1 is the identity and composition reference. Redraw THIS SAME coral-haired anime adventurer portrait as exemplary professionally hand-authored EXTREMELY LOW RESOLUTION pixel art, with the visual information budget of a native 32 by 32 pixel canvas. Preserve the same frontal bust framing, coral bob haircut and cowlick, golden star hair clip on viewer-right, large turquoise eyes, closed gentle smile, navy coat with gold trim, cream shirt, brown straps and red neck scarf. Preserve age, expression and face proportions, no chibi enlargement, no change of pose or crop. Pixel art must use EXACTLY a 32-column by 32-row virtual square grid across the entire square image, enlarged with nearest neighbor. Render on a 1024 by 1024 square so every logical pixel is an EXACT 32 by 32 solid-color square, at coordinates aligned to multiples of 32. No visual detail smaller than one logical pixel anywhere; NO half pixels, NO outlines finer than one logical cell, NO anti-aliasing, NO smoothed edges, NO gradients, NO dithering, NO texture. A single 32x32 portrait image only, NO labels, NO comparison panels, NO border, NO grid lines. Use at most 20 flat coordinated colors with a solid warm gray-beige background matching the reference. Editorial simplification is essential: coherent 3-5 large hair locks with intentional highlights instead of noisy small strands; the complete hair silhouette and cowlick remain legible; eye glyphs around 3x3 logical cells with one strategically placed white glint and teal pupil, readable eyelids with selective plum-dark accents; a deliberate 2-3 logical-pixel closed smile instead of a blurry salmon rectangle; a crisp tiny star-like gold clip made of a few cells; clear collar/scarf/strap hierarchy. Selective colored outlines: concentrate contrast on eyes, key hair separations and scarf, omit redundant internal lines. Crisp readable shape clusters, no blur or washed-out averaged color fringe. It must unmistakably look like an excellent 32x32 artist-drawn portrait when viewed small, not a detailed 64px or 128px pixel illustration. Do not add ornament, text, watermark or new details.
```

实际返回 1254×1254，未满足原生网格要求，落格后五官表达不足，随后进行了第三次调用。

## 2. 64 px concept

Input: `public/illustration.png`. Output: `ai-reference-64-raw.png`.

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: visual target for a high-resolution illustration to pixel-art algorithm. Input image 1 is the same-character identity and composition reference. Create a new professionally hand-drawn LOW-RESOLUTION 64x64 pixel portrait of exactly this coral-haired anime adventurer, the same front-facing bust crop, same age and gentle closed smile, same bob and upward cowlick, viewer-right golden star hairclip, turquoise eyes, navy gold-trim coat, cream shirt, brown straps and red scarf. Do not redesign the character or enlarge the eyes, do not change framing. Output a square pixel-art reference only: no labels, border, grid overlay or UI. The ENTIRE image is a uniform virtual 64 by 64 square pixel grid. Render every logical pixel as one solid square of equal size aligned to this grid. There must be absolutely no marks thinner than one logical pixel and no subpixel curves. Crisp nearest-neighbor edges, no texture, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, at most 24 flat colors total including a flat warm gray-beige background. Make the design readable at actual tiny display size. Carefully design the cluster shapes: 5 to 7 coherent large hair locks instead of dozens of fragmentary strands, 2 to 3 purposeful highlight clusters; selective burgundy hair separation and lower-silhouette lines, not a heavy continuous dark rim; coherent 1-pixel stair steps on the hair silhouette with no muddy mixed-color fringe; the golden clip is recognizable and has clear negative space; eye glyphs about 6 to 7 logical pixels wide with turquoise iris, dark upper eyelid, tiny pupil, single white glint, eyebrows consistent with the source; a well placed 4-pixel-wide closed subtle smile and tiny nose, no pink block for mouth; face/jaw silhouette is calm and intentional; clear collar/scarf/strap separation. Keep light colors clean and warm. Do not indiscriminately delete all lines: retain only those that explain overlapping forms and facial expression. Retain the source's identity and proportions, do not invent anatomy or costume details. This is a pixel artist's deliberate redraw, not a blurred downscale or a high-detail 128x128 illustration masquerading as low resolution.
```

实际返回 1254×1254，之后规范化为真实 64×64 并映射到当前软件同一色板。

## 3. Revised 32 px concept

Input: first `ai-reference-32-grid.png` (the first version is retained as `ai-reference-32-first-attempt-grid.png`). Output: `ai-reference-32-v2-raw.png`.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image 1 is an ACTUAL 32x32 pixel portrait. Improve this existing 32x32 sprite without adding any resolution or extra fine details. Keep exactly its 32x32 logical grid and the same composition and proportions. You may render a nearest-neighbor enlargement, but every solid logical cell must stay aligned to the original 32 by 32 grid. IMPORTANT: the original entire face is only about 14 logical pixels wide, each eye has a 3x3 logical-pixel budget. Do not add subpixel shapes or convert it to a 64px sprite. Preserve this same coral bob-haired girl, cowlick, right-side golden hairclip, front-facing bust, navy/gold coat, cream shirt, brown shoulder straps and red scarf, on flat warm gray-beige. Make the following few crucial edits on the logical pixel grid: (1) Make each eye a legible 3x3 glyph: top row 3 dark plum pixels for upper lid; middle row one white cell, one dark pupil cell, one teal cell; bottom row one pale skin cell and two saturated turquoise cells. Both eyes at consistent height, looking forward with a gentle cheerful expression. (2) Add a clearly visible tiny gentle CLOSED smile centered in the face, TWO adjacent plum-brown logical cells, with clean skin all around. Remove the prominent square nose so it does not read as a misplaced mouth. The smile must be visible at native 32px display, not faint pink. (3) Simplify the hair into 4-5 connected coral/pink clusters with a single light accent cluster and a few selective dark red separations, remove scattered stray dark one-cell noise. Keep the face boundary clean without a heavy uniformly dark jaw. (4) Simplify collar and scarf into distinct connected shapes; no fine costume textures. At most 16 flat colors. No gradients, no blending, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no dithering, no textures, no grid overlay, no letters, no border. The main goal is an excellent readable genuinely chunky 32x32 pixel portrait using purposeful big blocks, NOT a detailed higher-resolution pixel-art illustration. Preserve all unmodified logical pixel positions as much as possible. Use the same flat background color across the whole background, no vignette or shadows.
```

实际输出仍为 1254×1254，有服饰/头脸比例改写；再次规范化后用于最终 32×32 的设计讨论。
